# DJ Library

A personal music library connects online curation and acquired recordings with DJ preparation in Rekordbox.

## Language

**Library track**:
A recording recognized as one shared item in the personal library, regardless of how many playlists contain it. Different edits or mixes are separate library tracks.
_Avoid_: Playlist copy, download

**Audio file**:
The local audio belonging to a library track. It is distinct from the online entry that requested it.

**Source entry**:
A provider's identified track listing, such as a Spotify track or a SoundCloud upload. Different source entries may refer to the same recording, but similar names alone do not establish that relationship.

**Acquisition source**:
The identified location from which audio was actually obtained. It can differ from the source entry that prompted acquisition.
_Avoid_: Source, when the distinction matters

**Followed playlist**:
An online playlist selected for ongoing updates into the personal library.
_Avoid_: Folder

**Mirrored playlist**:
The Rekordbox counterpart of a followed playlist. Confirmed online memberships and their relative order are governed by the online playlist; unmatched local memberships remain user-owned. Unresolved entries can make the mirror incomplete.

**Reconciliation**:
The initial, reviewable process that establishes which entries in an online playlist correspond to which existing Rekordbox collection entries before online ownership is applied.

**Playlist membership**:
A library track's inclusion in a playlist, independent of its inclusion in any other playlist.

**Preparation**:
The cues, beatgrids, analysis, ratings, and other DJ work associated with a track in Rekordbox.

**Unresolved entry**:
A requested source entry that cannot yet be made available as intended and needs a retry, a user decision, or replacement audio.

**Confirmed match**:
An established relationship between a source entry and a library track that can be reused in future updates.

**Suggested match**:
A unique artist/title/compatible-duration candidate presented for approval. It is not a confirmed relationship until approved.

**Local-only membership**:
An entry already present in a mirrored Rekordbox playlist that has no confirmed relationship to the online playlist. It remains until explicitly removed.

**Library snapshot**:
A recoverable version of the personal library, including its audio, preparation, playlists, and source relationships.
_Avoid_: Database copy, when referring to a complete backup
